THE TEAM

Four makers, one boat

A small boat needs four things to stay pointed at something: a hand on the wheel, a line on the chart, a light at the top, and an ear in the dark. Each of us took one. Take a turn at any of them and watch the porthole: the sea out there answers to whoever you are standing next to.

STATION 01 · THE HELM

Chen See Ng

Creative Lead & Developer

Nobody watches the helm. You only notice it when the boat is already somewhere. Chen See built the underwater world you’re standing in right now. She’s drawn to interfaces that don’t explain themselves, the ones you have to feel your way through. When something breaks, she talks to it out loud, usually along the lines of “why are you doing this again,” followed by muttering at herself for being careless.

STATION 02 · THE CHART DESK

Elin Kok Jia Yi

Story Creator and Narrative Structure

The chart is drawn before the water is crossed, and it is the only reason anyone gets home. Elin is the story creator for Somewhere Still website. She is responsible for the website’s overall narrative structure, weaving together the characters, memories and interactive experiences into a single story, telling a story about love, loss and remembrance. She believes every memory deserves to be heard, even the one slowly fading away.

STATION 03 · THE MASTHEAD

Emily Blum

Concept Artist & Illustrator

The light at the masthead is not for the one holding it. It is so the rest of us can see. Emily is the concept artist for Somewhere Still. She develops the sketches and visual concepts for the website’s characters, environments, and story moments, helping shape the project’s overall artistic direction. When a drawing is not working, she will stare at it for far too long before asking, “why do you look wrong?” As if the sketch has an answer.

STATION 04 · THE SOUNDING

Fatih Kulualp

Music & Documentation

Past the reach of any lamp, you find the bottom by listening for it. Fatih created all audio for the project, from theme songs to UI sounds, and production. He also handled the documentation and research. He believes memorable melodies paired with emotional weight make for some good music, and prefers maximalist compositions. He needs to cool down on philosophizing, according to testimonials.

Still here. Somewhere still.
